About Me

February 25, 2020

I am a Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Connecticut. My research focuses on the area of arithmetic geometry, which is at the crossroads of number theory and algebraic geometry.

My professional website can be found at https://alozano.clas.uconn.edu/

I have also written a couple of number theory textbooks that can be found here at the AMS bookstore.
