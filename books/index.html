Number Theory and Geometry

An Introduction to Arithmetic Geometry

American Mathematical Society · Pure and Applied Undergraduate Texts, vol. 35 · 2019 · 488 pp · ISBN 978-1-4704-5016-8

An introduction to number theory in which geometry motivates the theorems: finding the integer and rational points on lines, conics and cubic curves leads to the fundamental theorem of arithmetic, quadratic reciprocity, continued fractions and Pell's equation, and finally elliptic curves. For a first course in number theory, or a junior–senior course in arithmetic geometry.

Elliptic Curves, Modular Forms, and Their L-functions

American Mathematical Society · Student Mathematical Library, vol. 58 (IAS/Park City Mathematical Subseries) · 2011 · 195 pp · ISBN 978-0-8218-5242-2

An introductory survey of the three families of objects in the title and the surprising connections between them, told with the number theory in front: the modularity theorem and Fermat's Last Theorem, the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture, and many concrete examples. Written for advanced undergraduates; it motivates definitions and explains the main theorems rather than proving them.
